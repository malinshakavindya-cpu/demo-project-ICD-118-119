eTaxCalc

eTax Calc is a tax calculator built for Sri Lankan taxpayers. It simplifies income tax, PAYE, and business tax calculations with a clean, mobile-friendly interface and step-by-step guidance. Ideal for students, freelancers, and beginners, it offers instant results, Sinhala tooltips, and downloadable summaries — making tax filing easier, clearer, and stress-free.

💸Payable Tax Calculator

The Payable Tax Calculator helps users estimate the monthly income tax deducted from their salary based on Sri Lanka’s progressive tax brackets. It applies tiered rates depending on the salary range, ensuring accurate and transparent tax breakdowns.

💸Income Tax Calculator

The Income Tax Calculator helps users estimate their annual tax obligations based on Sri Lanka’s progressive income tax slabs. It applies tiered rates to different portions of the annual income, ensuring accurate and transparent tax breakdowns.

//

💸 SSCL Tax Calculator

The SSCL Tax Calculator helps users compute the total tax payable under the Social Security Contribution Levy (SSCL), which includes both Sale Tax and Value Added Tax (VAT). This module ensures accurate calculation of indirect taxes applied to goods and services.

//

💸 Leasing Calculator

The Leasing Calculator helps users estimate monthly installment payments and explore loan options based on interest rates and repayment periods. It uses the standard EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) formula to compute leasing costs and supports reverse calculations to determine the maximum loan amount based on a given monthly payment.

💸Description about sri lankan tax

Sri Lanka’s tax system includes income tax, VAT, SSCL, and other levies managed by the Inland Revenue Department. Taxes are applied progressively based on income and transactions to support national development and public services.
