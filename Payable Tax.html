Payable Tax
    Payable Tax refers to the total amount of tax that must be paid on a transaction. In this SSCL calculator, it includes:
  • Sale Tax (2.5%): A fixed percentage applied directly to the original amount.
  • VAT (15%): Calculated on the amount after adding Sale Tax.
  • SSCL Tax Total: The sum of Sale Tax and VAT.
  • The Payable Tax is the total of these two components. It represents the tax burden added to the original amount and helps users understand how much extra they are paying due to SSCL regulations.
