WithHolding Tax Calculation

Withholding Tax (WHT) in Sri Lanka is a tax deducted at the source of income—such as interest, dividends, rent, royalties, and service fees—before the recipient receives payment. As of April 1, 2025, new Inland Revenue Act updates have revised rates and broadened coverage.
